# Code of Conduct

100xMatch is a project about bringing developers together, so we want our community to be a friendly, welcoming place for everyone. This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient and welcoming, especially to first-time contributors
- Respecting different opinions, experiences and skill levels
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Owning mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances. Yes, even though this is a "dating-style" app: it's a project, not a dating venue
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as an address or email, without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@Jatin4224](https://github.com/Jatin4224), privately on GitHub. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code of conduct. Depending on how serious it is, they may give a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project.
